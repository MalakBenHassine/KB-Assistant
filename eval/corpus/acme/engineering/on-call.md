# On-call rotation

## Rotation

The on-call rotation is weekly and hands over every Monday at 10:00 CET.
An engineer is on call for at most two weeks per quarter.

## Compensation

Each on-call week is paid with an on-call stipend of 300 EUR.
Time spent handling a page outside working hours is recovered as time off.

## Handover

The outgoing engineer writes a handover note listing open incidents and risky changes.
