# Incident response

## Severity levels

SEV1 means customers cannot use the product; SEV2 means a major feature is degraded;
SEV3 is a minor issue with a workaround.

## Paging

For a SEV1 incident, the on-call engineer must acknowledge the page within 5 minutes.
An incident commander is named for every SEV1 and SEV2 incident; the commander
coordinates and does not debug.

## Communication

During a SEV1 incident the status page is updated at least every 30 minutes.
Customer support is informed through the #incident-comms channel.

## Postmortems

A blameless postmortem is written for every SEV1 and SEV2 incident within
5 business days. Action items get an owner and a due date in the tracker.
