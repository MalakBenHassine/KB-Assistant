# Leave policy

## Paid leave

Employees get 25 days of paid leave per year, accrued monthly.
Up to 5 unused days of paid leave can be carried over, and carried-over days expire on March 31.

## Parental leave

Parental leave is 16 weeks at full pay for every parent.
Parental leave is open to employees after six months of employment.

## Sick leave

Employees can self-certify sick leave for up to 3 days; a doctor's note is needed from the fourth day.
