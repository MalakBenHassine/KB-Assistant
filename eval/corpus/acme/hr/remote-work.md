# Remote work policy

## Hybrid schedule

Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week; team days on site are agreed per team.

## Home office

Every employee receives a one-time home office budget of 500 EUR for a desk, a chair or a screen.

## Working from abroad

Working from abroad is allowed for up to 30 days per year, with the manager's approval
and a check by HR on tax and insurance rules.
