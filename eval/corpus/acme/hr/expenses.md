# Travel and expenses

## Submitting expenses

Expense reports are submitted within 30 days of the expense, with receipts attached.
Expenses above 200 EUR need the manager's approval before they are incurred.

## Meals and travel

Meals during business travel are reimbursed up to 40 EUR per day.
Flights shorter than 6 hours are booked in economy class.
Train is preferred over flights for trips under 4 hours.
