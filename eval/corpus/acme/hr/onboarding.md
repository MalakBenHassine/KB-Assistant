# Onboarding

## Before the first day

The laptop is shipped to new hires 5 days before their start date.
Accounts are created by IT two days before the start date.

## First months

Every new hire is assigned an onboarding buddy from another team.
The manager and the new hire agree on a 30-60-90 day plan in the first week.
The probation period lasts 6 months.
